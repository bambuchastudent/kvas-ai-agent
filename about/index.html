About KVASSISTENT

A human-first AI companion for making real homemade kvass from bread.

What it is

KVASSISTENT guides one real batch at a time. The person prepares the ingredients, observes the jar, smells and tastes the drink; the assistant remembers state, explains risk and asks for the next observation.

What makes it different

  • Six-language interface on one stable homepage.
  • Offline live-batch companion with structured JSON handoff.
  • Explicit safety stops instead of pretending AI can inspect food.
  • Open source, free to use and built in public.

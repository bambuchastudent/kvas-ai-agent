How KVASSISTENT works

From dry bread to a chilled bottle, with one clear next step at a time.

1. Start a batch

Enter volume, ingredients, start time and temperature.

2. Report what you observe

Surface, smell, taste, sunlight, temperature and bottle pressure stay explicit; unknown data stays unknown.

3. Receive the next safe step

The companion suggests one action and a check-in time. Mold, slime, dangerous smell or overheating stop the household protocol.

4. Share feedback

Ideas, recipes and photos can be sent through the website or Telegram and become structured work for a later release.

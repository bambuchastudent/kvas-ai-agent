One-line description

KVASSISTENT is a human-first AI companion that guides people through making homemade kvass while keeping observation, safety and final judgment in human hands.

Project facts

  • Free and open source.
  • Progressive web app with six languages.
  • Live batch state, safety flags and JSON handoff.
  • Public immutable releases plus a stable latest URL.
  • Telegram feedback and community recipe intake.
